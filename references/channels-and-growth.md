# Channels & Growth

How to choose acquisition channels and build growth loops for a SaaS product.

**Core principle:** Channel choice follows the product's motion and the funnel math — not what's popular. Recommend a channel because it reaches the right buyer at a cost the unit economics support.

---

## Channel selection logic

Work through these questions before recommending any channel:

1. **What's the motion?**
   - Product-led/self-serve → scalable, low-touch channels (SEO, content, product virality, communities)
   - Sales-led → targeted demand-gen (paid LinkedIn/search, ABM, outbound-supporting content, events)

2. **Where does the buyer already look?**
   - High search intent → SEO / search ads (people searching "link in bio free" are ready to try)
   - No explicit intent → social/influencer (interrupt with sharp creative, or skip)

3. **Does the math work?**
   - Judge channels on **cost-per-activated-user**, not cost-per-click
   - CAC must stay well under LTV (see metrics reference)

4. **Can the team actually run it?**
   - 2-person team running 5 channels runs all of them badly
   - Recommend 1–3 and sequence the rest

## Channel breakdown

### SEO / Organic search
- **Best for:** Products with clear search demand ("link shortener", "bio page free", "cara buat microsite")
- **Timeline:** Slow — 3–6 months to mature, compounding after that
- **Tactics:** Target high-intent keywords, programmatic/template pages (one per use case or integration), comparison/alternative pages
- **Watch out:** Don't kill it at week 6; it looks like failure early and like the best decision at month 9

### Content marketing
- **Best for:** Buyers who research before buying; feeds SEO + social + email simultaneously
- **Match content to funnel stage:**
  - Top (awareness): education, "how to X"
  - Mid (consideration): comparison, alternatives, use cases
  - Bottom (decision): customer stories, ROI calculators

### Paid acquisition
- **Search ads:** Capture existing intent, fast signal, instant on/off — but stops when spend stops
- **Social ads:** Create demand for audiences with no existing intent — works with strong creative
- **Rule:** Always tie to CAC payback, not vanity clicks. Validate message before scaling spend.

### Product-led / viral loops
- **The highest-leverage channel for PLG products and the most underexploited**
- Every public artifact the product creates is a distribution surface:
  - A shared short link → someone clicks → lands on the product → signs up
  - A published bio page with "made with [product]" badge → discovery
  - An invite mechanic → existing user brings in new user
- **Map the loop explicitly:**
  ```
  User creates artifact → Artifact seen by non-users → Some click through
  → Some sign up → They create artifacts → Loop repeats
  ```
- Optimize each step: more artifacts created, more visible branding on artifacts, lower-friction sign-up from artifact referral

### Communities & partnerships
- **Creator-facing tools:** Influencer/creator adoption is the channel — one creator with 100K followers who features the product converts better than any ad campaign
- **Integrations:** Co-marketing with adjacent tools borrows their audience and creates distribution
- **Timeline:** Slower to set up, durable once running

### Owned email/audience
- Not acquisition, but the cheapest re-acquisition — build the list from day one
- See lifecycle reference for onboarding and lifecycle use

## Building a growth plan

When asked "how do we grow", deliver:

1. **Goal + metric** — e.g., "double activated sign-ups in Q3"
2. **2–3 prioritized bets** — for each:
   - Which channel
   - Why it fits (motion + buyer + math)
   - Which funnel metric it moves
   - Rough effort and realistic timeframe
3. **Sequence** — what to start now vs. next quarter (e.g., "start paid for fast signal while SEO compounds")
4. **What to ignore** — explicitly name deprioritized channels so focus is clear
5. **Measurement** — leading indicator watched weekly + lagging metric that proves it worked

## Prioritization frameworks

Use when sorting many options:

| Framework | Use when |
|---|---|
| **ICE** (Impact, Confidence, Ease) | Quick gut-ranked list, fast decision |
| **RICE** (Reach, Impact, Confidence, Effort) | Can estimate reach numerically, more rigorous |

## Common traps — flag these proactively

- **Chasing channels before activation works.** If sign-ups don't reach first value, more sign-ups won't help. Fix activation first.
- **Vanity over substance.** Impressions and followers are not sign-ups. Always push the metric down the funnel.
- **Copying a competitor's playbook with a different motion.** A sales-led competitor's LinkedIn strategy may be wrong for a PLG product.
- **Abandoning compounding channels too early.** SEO and content look like failures at week 6 and like the best decision at month 9.
- **Scaling spend before validating conversion.** Spend money on acquisition only after the activated sign-up → paid conversion is proven.
